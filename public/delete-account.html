Delete Your Pressure Log Account

You can delete your data and account in two ways:

Option 1 — Delete instantly inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Pressure Log app and sign in
  2. Tap "Delete account" near the top of the Home screen
  3. Type DELETE to confirm

This immediately and permanently deletes all your blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, pulse, and walk records, and signs you out.

Option 2 — Request full account removal by email

The in-app option above deletes all of your health data right away. If you'd also like your login credentials (email address on file) fully removed from our system, email us and we'll process it within a few business days.

Email us to request deletion

What gets deleted

  • All blood pressure readings
  • All blood sugar readings
  • All weight & BMI records
  • All pulse scan records
  • All walk history
  • Your account login (email-based request only)
